I am a Software Engineer and Computing Technology graduate from Addis Ababa University, passionate about building scalable, reliable, and impactful technology solutions.

My experience spans full-stack software development, mobile application development, AI-powered systems, enterprise platforms, healthcare technology, telemedicine, land management, visitor management, IVR and contact center systems, and network infrastructure.

I have designed, developed, integrated, and contributed to real-world systems used by organizations and institutions across different domains. My work focuses on transforming complex business and operational requirements into practical, scalable, and production-ready digital solutions.
